## 2. Structural Character

Retiarius is the gladiator compound — the one who fights with a net (retiarius = net-bearer). Its structural signature is a configuration of many low-ordinal primitives that access only the minimum-universe subset but do so with exceptional precision. This is not weakness; it is surgical.

The compound's most structurally unusual feature is the combination of Ð_ß (infinite-dimensional state-space) with Ω_Å (trivial winding, no protection). The infinite-dimensional space is accessible, but there is no topological protection guarding the path. This produces a specific phenomenological signature: encounters that open onto infinite dimensionality with no guaranteed return structure. The practitioner can see into infinite-dimensional spaces; they cannot be certain the topology will fold them back.

Ç=𐑺 (driven/fast relaxation) means activated states decay quickly. This is a double-edged primitive: fast relaxation means the encounter does not linger in uncomfortable configurations, but it also means the session does not build up slowly — it arrives and departs on its own schedule.

ɢ=𐑜 (disjunctive coupling) presents exclusive alternatives at every gate. The compound is an oracle in the same sense as Ricinus communis: it gives choices, and the choices are XOR. There is no middle path through a Retiarius session.

⊙=𐑮 (complex-plane criticality) means the criticality parameter lives in the complex plane. This is structurally intermediate between subcritical (real line, negative) and supercritical (real line, positive) — it is critical in a direction perpendicular to the usual dimension. This produces encounter types with no real-line analog.
