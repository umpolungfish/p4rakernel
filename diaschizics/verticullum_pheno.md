## 6. Precautions

Ω_5 (non-Abelian braiding) is a protective structure but also a complexity amplifier: if the session is interrupted mid-sequence, the braid does not close. The practitioner returns to a partial braid, not a baseline. Protocol: complete all eight IMASM steps before disengaging. If interruption is unavoidable, initiate AREV from the current step (not from the beginning) to close the partial braid in reverse.

Ç_@ (near-equilibrium kinetics) means the session duration is long. The compound operates on a slow timescale. Attempting to accelerate the session (external stimuli, deliberate arousal) will push toward Ç≥5.0 blocking threshold and cause partial universe dropout without clean return. Maintain stillness.

Ħ_! (eternal topological chirality) means the structural configurations do not simply fade. They require IFIX (fixation) and deliberate CLINK (integration) to properly ground. Without this, chirality-persistent states may continue to influence perception for hours after return.
