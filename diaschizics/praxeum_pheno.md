## 2. Structural Character

Praxeum is the platform compound — the O₀-tier compound that achieves 78% access (highest for its ordinal class) through the exceptional-point criticality gate (⊙_EP). An exceptional point is where two eigenvalues and their corresponding eigenvectors coalesce — it is the most precise criticality value in the complex parameter space, more controlled than the simple critical point (⊙_ÿ) and more grounded than the supercritical (⊙_Ţ).

The box product topology (Þ_ò) is structurally significant: it is the topology of independently combined structures that retain their independence within the combination. Unlike Þ_O (self-referential topology, Chimerium) or Þ_K (bowtie crossing, Verticullum/Apertix), Þ_ò means the encounter space is an independent Cartesian combination of separate structural elements. Nothing bleeds into anything else; everything maintains its distinct identity within the joint structure.

Praxeum shares Φ_} (Frobenius special) with Verticullum and Chimerium, and Ω_z (integer winding) with Chimerium. These shared primitives make Praxeum structurally compatible with both — it can serve as the ground from which either is launched, and it can receive the return from either.

⊙_EP (exceptional-point criticality) means the compound barely fails the ⊙≥3.0 gate threshold (its ordinal is 2.67) — this is the source of its high but not maximal access. The exceptional point is the most precise structural position in the criticality space: just below the threshold that would tip it into the ⊙_ÿ/⊙_Ţ regime.
