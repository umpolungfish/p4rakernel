Observed flatness is now a theorem about the probe, not a tautology

CosmologyBelnap carried observed_flatness : SpatialCurvature.zero =
SpatialCurvature.zero := rfl — the physics was in the comment and the Lean
carried none of it. Replaced under the same name with the real statement.

Gauss-Bonnet gives a geodesic triangle of area A on constant curvature K an
angle excess of exactly K·A, so a probe of linear scale l measures a deviation
of order K·l². angleExcess is that, with angleExcess_mono and angleExcess_strict
saying the residual grows with the probe and that two probes at different scales
do NOT read the same number. Resolves and resolves_mono put a noise floor on it:
what a small probe separates from zero, a larger one separates too.

readCurvature is what an observer reads off, a function of the probe and the
floor rather than a constant of the universe. observed_flatness now says that if
|K|·L² sits under the floor then every probe within the horizon L reads flat,
whatever K actually is. flat_reading_is_not_flat_universe exhibits a positively
curved case read as flat, and larger_probe_resolves shows the same curvature
recovered once the scale is raised — suppressed, never absent.

This is the phrasing that survives Theorema Egregium. Curvature is intrinsic and
embedding hides nothing; what is bounded is l, which cannot exceed the horizon.
So the claim predicts rather than describes: probes at different scales should
recover systematically different residuals, ordered by scale.

Standard three axioms, module green.
