Two more axioms down, and this time by a different defect

bowtie_max_three_layers and box_irreducible in Decomposition also proved False,
but not the way A through D did. Both hypotheses constrained both variables, so
they looked sound. The defect was a level down, in validTemporalDecomposition:
its wool branch does not mention n at all, because infinite memory means
arbitrary decomposition — which is the INTENDED reading. So for a wool system
the hypothesis holds for every n, and a universal bound on n is refuted by
picking a bigger one. n = 4 against a bound of 3, n = 2 against a claim of 1.

The outcomes differ and that is the part worth keeping. Excluding wool, the
bowtie bound is a THEOREM: maxTemporalLayers is 1, 2 or 3 on the bounded
chiralities, so it follows by cases with nothing assumed. Box irreducibility
does NOT follow even then, because chiralities permitting 2 and 3 layers remain,
so n = 1 is a genuine constraint on the system rather than a consequence of the
definitions, and it is now a decidable predicate discharged per system.

An axiom that becomes a theorem and an axiom that becomes an obligation are both
better than an axiom, and they are not the same result. Collapsing them would
have hidden which is which.

Six axioms now, two defects. The first four had a free variable in the
consequent. These two had a hypothesis that was vacuously true. Both shapes look
like ordinary implications until you ask what the antecedent actually pins down.
