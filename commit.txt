Erdos-Straus: the ceiling holds to four million

The block from 2*10^6 to 4*10^6 tops out at rung 55, below the record 71 at
1430641, so the least closing rung never exceeds 71 over everything read. The
ratio to n^(1/4) runs 1.4 to 2.1 past a million and peaks at 4.6 at n = 1201, so
the shape holds and the constant is set by the small values.
