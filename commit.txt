Erdos-Straus: the searched rung, as a condition on a group

For a frontier value the divisor has to come from the primes of M = na, so the
criterion turns into a question about the multiplicative group mod r. The
divisors of M^2 reduce to a reachable set R, the rung closes when -M is in it,
and since M is itself in R the content is whether -1 is in the subgroup the
primes of M generate.

straus_of_neg_one_divisor is the half that needs no exponent bookkeeping: a
divisor t of M with t = -1 (mod r) closes the rung by u = M*t.
negMReachable_of_negOneReachable states it against the reachable-set predicate.

The other half is not an equivalence, and SubgroupExceedsReach names why: the
subgroup allows any exponent, the divisors of M^2 cap them at twice those of M.
Over the 162 frontier values below 20000, -1 in the subgroup predicts closure at
248 of 251 rungs. All three misses are the same shape — n = 1201 at r = 11, and
n = 2521 at r = 11 and r = 19 — where M is squarefree, the primes generate the
whole group, and -1 needs a higher power than M^2 supplies.
