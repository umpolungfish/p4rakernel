The size-Ramsey number, from the object that forked it

The second ob3ect of the nest reads size-Ramsey as a fork whose two arms must
both close: one constructs a host that forces, the other establishes that no
smaller host does. HostForces is the first arm's content, ForcingSizes is the set
it lands in, sizeRamsey is the infimum, and sizeRamsey_le is the upper bound a
construction gives outright. The second arm — the infimum attained rather than
merely bounded — is not free, and the file says so rather than assuming it.

Same separation as the Ramsey pair: the infimum and the non-emptiness are
distinct, since an infimum over an empty set is zero and would read here as "no
edges needed".
