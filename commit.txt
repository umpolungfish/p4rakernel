The phase lattice is proved, not computed.

The winding manuscript's central claim was arithmetic run in Python and reported
as a table. It is a theorem and it is small, so here it is.

eigenvalues_generate. In ZMod 10, where n stands for the winding n/10, the
subgroup generated by the braid generator's two eigenvalues, 4/10 and -3/10, is
the whole of the tenths. So the phases the model builds from its own generator
are exactly the tenths: no finer and no coarser. The step is that 4 and 7 sum to
11, which is 1.

framing_order_ten. The framing phase has order exactly ten, ten of them closing
the turn and no fewer, which is the period the T(2,n) torus family exhibits.
Stated with the minimality rather than just the closure, since the closure alone
would hold for any divisor.

eighth_not_a_tenth and quarter_not_a_tenth. T is one eighth of a winding and S
is one quarter, and neither is a multiple of a tenth: 1/8 = p/10 forces 10 = 8p.

half_is_a_tenth. Z is one half and IS on the lattice, 1/2 = 5/10. Kept because
it is what makes the obstruction specific: an argument excluding T from the
lattice alone must not also exclude Z, and this shows the separation is real
rather than an artifact of how the question was posed.

The scope is deliberately narrow and the docstring says so. This says nothing
about whether some braid WORD realizes T, since eigenvalues of a product of
non-commuting matrices need not lie in the group generated by the factors'
eigenvalues, and if they did the image could not be dense. What is proved is
that the phase lattice does not contain the gate.

Zero sorries. Axiom dependencies printed in the file rather than left to the
reader: propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound, and nothing else.
