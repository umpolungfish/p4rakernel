Sixty-two axioms in the SIC moduli files were never assumptions

The d=16 and d=20 moduli modules carried twenty-seven and thirty-five axioms
between them, and none of them was a mathematical assumption. They were recorded
PARI results wearing the syntax of one.

Almost all came in pairs: an opaque constant and a second axiom giving its
value. A value is a value. Each pair is now a definition and a theorem proved by
rfl, which assumes nothing and says exactly what the pair said.

The tower degrees were seven separate assertions each. They are not independent:
at d=16 the tower doubles, then quadruples through the maximal-growth interval,
then doubles again once the leading invariant saturates, so only the seed is
data. At d=20 the odd part of the conductor contributes a factor of three at
k=4, so that tower does not obey a single ratio and the levels are given
directly. Either way every level is now checked rather than taken.

Three at d=20 said nothing at all. Two were axioms of True, which is provable
and carries no content, so what they recorded was a name and no claim. The third
was an opaque Prop asserted to hold. All three had real content in the prose
around them, and that content is decidable against the two dimension lists the
same file states: 20 fails the coinvariant identity, 16 and 24 hold it, and the
class group at 20 has order two exactly as at 16, so it cannot be what
distinguishes them.

Both modules build, and the theorems stand on nothing. The coinvariant
confirmation at d=16 and the class-group statement at d=20 depend on no axioms
whatever; the rest carry only propext and Quot.sound, which is what any use of
Mathlib's equality carries. The tower theorems use `decide` rather than
`native_decide`, so the compiler is out of the trusted base and the kernel
checks the arithmetic itself.

Repository axiom count falls from 435 to 373.
