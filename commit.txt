The other placeholders each carry the verdict they were standing next to.
BinomialGCD's B is a genuine opposition — h(n)→∞ along the primes is True and
h(n)→∞ through all n is False, one proposition under two quantifiers, exactly
the pair fsplit produces from B. SumsetAvoidingAP's is not: the claim is refuted
and the constant is open, F with N, which is not the T/F opposition B needs.

settled — so the verdict column records the refutation directly. And Polynom's
meta-level B for the divergence between two runs is N against T, since one run
read Q3 open and the other read it true; B needs one of them to have read it
