UNSOLVED: set theory, and a knot built from scratch

Mathlib carries cardinals, cofinality and strong limits, so GCH and SCH needed
only statements that say the problem. The source's GCH omitted the strictly-
between clause that is the whole content.

Knots needed building. A knot is a continuous injective periodic map into
three-space; ambient isotopy is a continuous family of homeomorphisms starting at
the identity; the round circle is written out; unknotted means isotopic to it.
The unknotting problem is then stated in the form that is open — decidable in
polynomial time — over a finite combinatorial diagram, since polynomial time
needs an input size and a continuous knot has none.

Baum-Connes and Novikov want characteristic classes and an assembly map that no
amount of local definition supplies honestly; they are named in the docstring as
waiting on that machinery rather than stated against constants that do not exist.

Forty-four statements, build green at 8593 jobs.
