reciprocal sum. It splits cleanly, and the combinatorial half is now proved.

dyadic_split: [1, 2^J) is the disjoint union of its dyadic blocks, by induction
on J through sum_Ico_consecutive.

block_sum_le: over one block the reciprocals are bounded by r₃(2^j)/2^j — at most
rothNumberNat(2^j) terms by block_card_le, each at most 1/2^j because the block
starts there. Progression-freeness passes to the subset by ThreeAPFree.mono.

summable_of_blocks: if Σ_j r₃(2^j)/2^j converges then so does the reciprocal sum.
Through summable_subtype_iff_indicator to an indicator on ℕ, then bounded partial

bound each block, compare to the tsum.
