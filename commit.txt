Erdos-Straus: the k-shift numbers, measured over the whole range

k=2 supplies the rung for 321 of the 624 frontier values below 200000, k=4 for
103, k=6 for 59, and k <= 40 for 589. The reparametrization is exact — every
cofactor is k*r-1 — but k is not bounded a priori and reaches (M+1)/r, which is
why the last 35 sit further out rather than outside the family.
