The three ErdosFormalize batches compile, and the sorries are gone

All three are rewritten against the Mathlib that exists and are in the build.
Together they carried 119 theorem statements and 76 sorries, and not one had
ever been checked: they imported Mathlib.Combinatorics.Ramsey.Basic and
Mathlib.Combinatorics.Hypergraph, neither of which has ever existed, and their
bodies used Rat.toReal, SimpleGraph.Cycle and SimpleGraph.CliqueNumber, which do
not either.

Open problems are Props now rather than sorried theorems. That is the whole
structural change and it is the one that matters: a sorry asserts its statement
and puts everything downstream on sorryAx, so a false statement among 119
unchecked ones would have been a live falsehood — which is not hypothetical,
since three of the neighbouring modules turned out to carry claims that were
refutable rather than merely unproved. A Prop says what the problem is and
claims nothing.

What could be proved is proved. Batch 1: squares below N number at most √N + 1,
so they have density zero; a proper k-colouring of n vertices leaves some class
with at least n/k of them, by fibering the colour map; K_n has n(n−1)/2 edges,
which is why decomposing it into few cycles is refuted; ∑1/n! converges and ∑1/p
diverges, both from Mathlib; there are infinitely many primes, which was one of
the sorried statements. Batch 2: submultiplicativity gives c(kn) ≤ c(n)^k, with
the empty-walk normalisation c(0)=1 made explicit, since submultiplicativity
alone permits c(0) above one; the ratio set of A has at most |A|² elements; and
three distinct primes never form an LCM triple. Batch 3: the extremal set is
bounded above by the number of pairs, so its supremum is genuine rather than the
junk value an unbounded sSup on ℕ returns.

Each batch ends with its verdict distribution computed from the table rather
than asserted beside it.

And the last Erdős sorry is gone with them. Classical/ErdosStrausDecomposition
carried class_one_exists as a sorry — n ≡ 1 (mod 4), the open heart of the
conjecture. A sorry asserts what it stands under, so that one asserted the
conjecture and put the file's three genuinely proved congruence classes on
sorryAx alongside it. It is ClassOneExists now, and conjecture_of_class_one
proves the whole conjecture follows from that single class, so the gap is
located rather than assumed away. The class is N, unknown, not F.

No Erdős file in the repository contains a sorry.
