Four covering families, and the surviving class is 1.80%

Each family fixes the divisor u in the master criterion and reads the rung off n,
so nothing is searched: the rung comes from a factorisation of n, n+1 or n+4, and
the three denominators are written from n and r.

  straus_of_divisor    u = d ≡ 2 (mod 3) dividing n(n+3)/4, rung 3 — the greedy step
  straus_prime_family  u = M: any prime p ∣ n with p ≡ 3 (mod 4) IS a rung, and the
                       two later denominators coincide, 4/n = 1/a + 1/b + 1/b
  straus_divisor_family u = a: rung any divisor of n(n+1) ≡ 3 (mod 4),
                       4/n = 1/a + 1/b + 1/(nb)
  straus_n_family      u = n: rung any divisor of n(n+4) ≡ 3 (mod 4), and the third
                       denominator is a times the second, c = a·b
