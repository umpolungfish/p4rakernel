MachHolonomy: transport is a connection, and the basepoint is a theorem

A word is a loop and reading it is transport around that loop. Three things
about that picture, machine-checked.

Transport composes along concatenation and rotation conjugates it rather than
fixing it: transport (t ++ v) (transport v s) = transport v (transport (v ++ t) s),
with no identity making the two readings equal. What does survive the basepoint
is the abelian image, the signed balance, and it survives because it factors
through a permutation.

Closure is balance zero AND no prefix dipping below zero. Balance alone is not
closure: [fsplit, ffuse] closes, its rotation [ffuse, fsplit] has the same
balance and dips at the first mark.

The cycle lemma: a word of balance zero always has a rotation that closes, and
the rotation is read off the word — cut where the prefix balance is least. The
prefix identities for a rotation are proved on both sides of the wrap, so the
reading point is determined by the word rather than chosen for it. That is what
makes a canonical basepoint a theorem instead of a convention.

Also: the conductor generator at d = 2^n is a power of sixteen exactly when
n + 1 is divisible by four, which is the one congruence carrying 16, 16^2, 16^3,
16^4 at d = 8, 128, 2048, 32768. The exponent counts n, so no member of the
family carries meaning the others do not.
