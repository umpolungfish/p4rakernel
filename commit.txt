Fix the submitted file rather than answer it with a different theorem

I diagnosed the Bloom–Sisask submission, proved a cleaner theorem beside it, and
called that a reply. It was not one. Handed broken Lean, the job is to make it
work.

So bloom_sisask_lemma is proved, under the name it arrived with, with its own
definitions — the Set-valued indicator, the ℂ-valued convolution, density,
additive_energy — and its own conclusion, E(A) ≥ α³·|G|. No sorry; propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound alone.

What the repair took. The definitions needed Decidable (x ∈ A) and a Finset in
place of Fintype ↑A, which is why nothing elaborated past line 8. Every `λ`
binder had to go: it is notation for `fun`, so `∀ λ : ℝ` and `intro λ` were
syntax errors, about six of them. Complex.norm_eq_abs does not exist and
div_le_div_iff is div_le_div_iff₀ now. `ring` does not close goals in an
AddCommGroup — that is `abel`.

The six sorries did not need discharging in the form they were written. Two of
them were an attempt to prove Hölder and an interpolation inequality by hand; the
argument does not need either. convolution_eq_reps is the bridge: the convolution
of indicators at x counts the representations of x as a sum of two elements, so
the ℂ-valued energy IS the ℕ-valued one, and energy_ge applies. The rest is
α³|G| ≤ |A|⁴/|G|, which holds because |A|·|G| ≥ 1.

The name stays as submitted so the file is fixed rather than replaced, and its
docstring says plainly what it is: the Cauchy–Schwarz bound on additive energy,
not the Bloom–Sisask theorem, and weaker than the |A|⁴/|G| it is derived from.
