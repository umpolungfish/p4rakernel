The cycle predicate the third batch lacked

The fourth ob3ect forks the graph space on the property itself: graphs carrying a
pair of edge-disjoint cycles, and graphs where every two cycles share an edge.
The second arm's edge count is the witness, and the extremal function is the
maximum over that arm.

So the predicate is written. A cycle is a vertex list of length at least three
with no repeats and every consecutive pair adjacent; its edges are the
consecutive pairs closed up; two cycles are edge-disjoint when neither
orientation of an edge of one appears in the other.
MaxEdgesEveryTwoCyclesMeet states the extremal count with both halves — the bound
and its attainment — and names the classical value.

The entry removed from this file bounded the edge count by 3n under the
hypothesis 3(n-1)/2 >= 0, which is true of every n.
