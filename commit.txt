Erdős's conjecture at every k, reduced — the 3-specific step was one lemma

The k=3 development runs on Mathlib's ThreeAPFree and rothNumberNat, and takes
translation invariance from addRothNumber_Ico. Neither exists for k ≥ 4, and that
lemma was the only thing in the argument that knew the length of a progression.

card_le_r_k proves it directly at every k. Shift a progression-free subset of any
interval down to range N; the shift is injective there, so the count is
preserved; a progression in the image lifts back by adding the offset, so the
image is still progression-free; and r_k is a supremum over exactly that family.

Everything downstream then generalises without change of substance. The dyadic
decomposition, the block estimate, the transport from a subtype to an indicator,
the comparison with a p-series — none of them mention k except through the
counting function that bounds it. erdos_conjecture_of_bound is the statement they
add up to: given r_k(N) ≤ C·N/(log N)^(1+ε) for some ε > 0, divergent reciprocals
force a k-term progression. Uniform in k, no axioms beyond propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

Which changes what is open. Before this the k ≥ 4 case was open mathematics with
an unstated shape; now the shape is a single inequality and everything on the
far side of it is machine-checked. A door is not open because it is a door.

The lift reads T on card_le_r_k, block_sum_le, summable_of_blocks and
erdos_conjecture_of_bound.
