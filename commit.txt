The three ErdosFormalize batches were written against a Mathlib that does not exist

ErdosFormalize, ErdosFormalize2 and ErdosFormalize3 are 2318 lines carrying 119
theorem statements and 76 sorries, and not one of them has ever been checked.
They import modules that are absent from this toolchain, and two —
Mathlib.Combinatorics.Ramsey.Basic and Mathlib.Combinatorics.Hypergraph — have
never existed in Mathlib at all. Collapsing the imports to plain Mathlib does
not rescue them: the bodies refer to Rat.toReal, SimpleGraph.Cycle and
SimpleGraph.CliqueNumber, none of which exist either, at a dozen errors per
hundred lines.

The sorry count is the smaller half of it. A sorry asserts its statement, so
adding these to the build would put every result in them on sorryAx, and a false
statement among them would be a live falsehood. That is not hypothetical: three
of the neighbouring modules carried claims that were refutable rather than
merely unproved — a distinct-distances function defined by an infimum that made
it identically zero, an LCM density claim pointing the wrong way, and a base
comparison that diverged for every n ≥ 2 — and all three had the same
provenance, a statement written down and never run.

Each file now says so at the top, with what is absent and what it would take.
Nothing is deleted. Making them real means rewriting 2318 lines against the
Mathlib that exists, which is a separate decision from the Erdős work in
Millennium/Erdos/ — that set is complete, checked, and in the build.
