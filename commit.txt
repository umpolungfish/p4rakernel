Erdos-Straus: the cofactor form, and the greedy rung as a prime condition

The witnesses the frontier uses are cofactors, not the M*t I wrote first: a
factorisation M = u*w with w = -1 (mod r), so M+u = u(w+1). That is
closedAtRung_of_cofactor, and it is the whole of what the frontier needs.

At rung 3 it becomes a statement about one prime. Every frontier value has
M = 1 (mod 3) — frontier_M_mod_three — so a divisor at 2 exists exactly when
some prime of M sits at 2 (mod 3), and that prime is the entire witness. That is
closedAtRung_three_of_prime, and it takes 873 of the 1271 frontier values below
200000 on its own.

Cascading the same test up the ladder leaves two values needing a divisor of M^2
rather than of M: 2521 at rung 23, already witnessed here, and 196561 at rung 27,
now witnessed as straus_196561. There M = 7^2*17*59*196561 and the divisor is
7^4*17 — the fourth power exists only after squaring, which is the exponent
budget being exercised rather than an accident.
