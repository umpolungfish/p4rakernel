The joint growth, fused because both readings are of the same primes

The eleventh ob3ect fuses lcm(1..n) and Landau's g(n) into one statement, and its
reason is why the fuse closes: both estimates come from the distribution of the
primes below n. log lcm(1..n) asymptotic to n, log g(n) asymptotic to
sqrt(n log n) — two readings of one layout, not two facts about unrelated
objects.

JointLogarithmicGrowth states both. lcmUpTo is the lcm of a finite set rather
than a fold, so the divisibility is the library's own lemma instead of an
induction over a fold's shape — my first version fought that shape and lost.
