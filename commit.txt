Erdos-Straus: the three statements, ordered

RungBounded and straus_class_of_rungBounded. A bound B with a closing rung below
B n for every n in the class gives the conjecture on that class outright.

The ordering is now explicit in the file. Weakest: every n has some rung, which
by ladder_complete is the conjecture itself. Middle: every n has a rung where M
rather than M^2 carries a divisor at -1 — strictly stronger, and 2521 inhabits
the gap. Strongest: the bound, consistent with C*n^(1/4) on everything measured,
and the one worth attacking since the rungs below a bound are finitely many and
each is decidable.
