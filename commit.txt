The key dissolve (overlap_deg.gp, banked in ~/imsgct/d12_sic_build): each overlap
O_{a,b} is individually a LOW-degree algebraic number (max degree 32 over Q; distribution
deg 2:7, 4:16, 8:32, 16:48, 32:40), even though the twelve coordinates jointly need the
degree-512 magnitude tower. So no compositum tower is needed: equiangularity splits into
143 small per-overlap identities.

Per overlap the pinned data (overlap_pin.gp, 1500-digit fiducial, floor gate >4700 bits,
all 143 PASS / 0 failures) is the monic irreducible min poly p of O_{a,b} plus the
lindep-recovered q with conj(O) = q(O) in the power basis of Q(O), pinned independently
of p. The Lean theorem equiangularity_all then checks, in exact rational polynomial
arithmetic (flat List Q engine, no floats, no square roots), that 13*x*q(x) == 1 mod p
for all 143 entries, i.e. 13*O*conj(O) = 1, i.e. |O|^2 = 1/13. covers_all_pairs pins
the index set to exactly the 143 non-identity WH pairs, no gaps, no duplicates. The
congruence is NOT true by construction: q was recovered from the numerics of conj(O) by
lindep, not computed as a modular inverse, so p and q are independent pinnings and the
exact identity closed on its own -- the same closure structure as norm_sum.
