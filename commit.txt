The Millennium theorems as OVMs, in the kernel, and two counts that do not hold

The paper's Verification section says the coordinate words and the Section 3
propositions are recorded in Lean and build without gaps. They are now.

OVMCatalog.lean is generated from IG_catalog.json and the two sample lists the
census was taken over. Nothing in it is typed by hand: the glyph-to-constructor
map is read out of Imscription.lean's own shavian definitions, so a rename in
the Lean cannot drift from a rename in the generator. seven_words_roundtrip
renders every structure back to the catalog word it came from.

OVMTaxonomy.lean carries the mathematics. The positivity criterion is proved
rather than stipulated: on one turn the self-inverse windings are exactly 0 and
1/2, so positivity is a fact about angles and not a separate axis. The
twenty-four combinations and the twenty distinct cells are counted by decide.
Yang-Mills leaves the winding line, and it is the only one of the seven that
does. Three of the seven theorems carry universal broadcast, and they are the
three the paper names.

Two of the paper's counts do not survive the check, and both are recorded in
the file rather than smoothed over.

The measurement inversion says six of the seven questions carry a Dirac
measure. Five do. The Yang-Mills question, catalog yang_mills_mass_gap, carries
the commutator like the theorems, so there are two exceptions and not one. The
shape of the claim stands; the count does not.

The convergence says seventy-nine of a hundred and fourteen settled objects
occupy the complete-negative-static cell. The recorded sample holds a hundred
and fifteen entries and seventy-five occupy it, or eighty once dynamics is
dropped. The concentration is real and the number is wrong.

Everything else reproduces exactly, the 25-entry table cell for cell and the
criticality gradient on both arms. No sorry, no new axiom: the standard three.
