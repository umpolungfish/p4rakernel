One lifter, per module, cross-checked against the axiom oracle

The proof lifter existed in twenty-four copies across p4ramill, in two
generations that disagreed on both things that matter. Twenty of them matched
the SPELLING of sorry — the bug my own ProofLift docstring records, where
sorryDepth and ym_has_stacked_not_parallel_sorries are fully proved, contain the
substring, and read B. None of those twenty had isAssumed at all, so a declared
axiom lifted to a commit and read exactly like a proved lemma. The corpus sweep
ran on that copy, which is a second reason its results were not worth keeping.

The lifter now lives once, at Imscribing/Vox/ProofLift.lean, in the lakefile, and
every sweep file imports it instead of restating it.

It sweeps per module, which is what worked before and what I had drifted away
from. Importing the whole corpus into one environment is blocked by name
collisions between modules, and the lift never needed it: it is per declaration,
so one module at a time means nothing has to coexist and the collisions stop
mattering. isGenerated drops the elaborator's own bookkeeping — _proof_, _simp_,
match_, eq_, sizeOf_spec, ctorIdx, the decidability instances, noConfusion, injEq
— which is not a claim the corpus makes and which buries the claims that are.

And the sweep now emits a third column: the axioms the declaration actually
rests on, from CollectAxioms. #print axioms knows nothing about the twelve, so it
is an independent oracle, and a sweep reporting only its own verdict cannot be
checked. sweep/run_sweep.sh runs the whole chain in the repo and writes the
cross-check beside the words.

On Problem0003 the two agree exactly, eighteen declarations, no disagreement in
either direction. B 2, N 11, T 5. Both B's are the assumed ones:
erdos_problem_3_k3_unconditional, resting on bloom_sisask_upper_bound, and
erdos_turan_ap_tier. Every clean declaration reads T or N, and the conditional
forms — erdos_problem_3_full, erdos_k3_certified — read N clean, because taking
the hypothesis is not an open fork.
