Szemeredi: define r_k, prove Behrend, and state the bounds about a real object

Six opaque symbols and eight axioms about them, two theorems that unwrapped an
axiom each. The trouble is `opaque r_k (k N : ℕ) : ℕ` — a constant with no body.
Every bound stated about it is a bound on an arbitrary function of two naturals,
satisfied by `fun _ _ => 0`. It cannot be contradicted and it cannot discharge
anything, which I found by trying to use it and being unable to.

r_k is defined now: the largest cardinality among progression-free subsets of
range N, a supremum over a finite family. r_k_le proves the definition has
content — such a subset has at most N elements — which is exactly the kind of
fact an opaque symbol admits none of.

Behrend at k = 3 stops being an axiom. Behrend.roth_lower_bound is in Mathlib and
ErdosTuranAP already re-exports it as a theorem; assuming it again here, in a
weaker form about an opaque symbol, was the same claim written twice with the
second copy inert.

Kelley–Meka and Bloom–Sisask are stated about rothNumberNat, the object Mathlib
defines. Bloom–Sisask is the weaker of the two and is stated separately because
it is the shape the consumer wants: r₃(N) ≤ C·N/(log N)^(1+c) is what
Problem0003.erdos_problem_3_k3_of_roth_bound takes.

So Erdős 3 at k=3 now closes in the corpus. erdos_problem_3_k3_unconditional
depends on propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound and one named axiom —
bloom_sisask_upper_bound — and nothing else. Every other theorem in Problem0003
depends on the foundational three alone.

The six density-increment axioms stay as they are, and the header now says what
they are: AP_count, gowers_norm, Nilsequence, nilseq_eval and nilseq_complexity
have no bodies, so those axioms carry the shape of Szemerédi's argument and none
of its force. They are an outline. Written down as such rather than left to read
like assumptions doing work.
