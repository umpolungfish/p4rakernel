71 at n = 1430641, so the ceiling of 59 I recorded from the first million was a
range fact, not the shape. Out to 2*10^6 the records are 3, 7, 11, 23, 31, 59, 71
and the ratio to n^(1/4) is 3.0, 3.5, 2.2, 4.6, 2.6, 3.3, 2.1 — largest at small
n, settling at 2.0 to 2.1 further out. So C*n^(1/4) fits with C just above 4.6,
and the binding constant comes from n = 1201.
