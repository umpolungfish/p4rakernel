The energy bound, proved — and it is not Bloom–Sisask

A file arrived headed "Bloom–Sisask Theorem - Complete Formalization". It is
neither, and the second part is checkable: it does not compile, and it fails at
line 8, before any proof is reached. `indicator` needs Decidable (x ∈ A) and
`density` needs Fintype ↑A on a Set coercion; neither instance arrives on its
own. Past that, `λ` is used as a binder about six times — it is notation for
`fun` in Lean 4, so `∀ λ : ℝ` and `intro λ` are syntax errors —
`Complex.norm_eq_abs` does not exist, `pow_eq_zero` is deprecated, and six
`sorry`s remain, including the two carrying every piece of the analytic content.

The naming matters more than the errors. Bloom–Sisask bounds a progression-free
subset of [N] by C·N/(log N)^{1+c}. The submitted statement is about additive
energy in a finite abelian group and contains no progression at all. Filing it
under that name in this corpus, a day after stating carefully what Bloom–Sisask
is, would have put a wrong citation next to a right one.

What it reaches for is real and is stronger than what it claims. Its conclusion,
E(A) ≥ α³|G|, is implied by the standard bound E(A) ≥ |A|⁴/|G| and falls short of
it by a factor of |A|·|G|. So the standard one is proved here instead:
representation counts sum to |A|² across |G| points, and squares cannot sum to
less than the square of the mean. Two theorems, no sorry, propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound alone.

Stated over Finset with ℕ-valued counts on purpose. The Set/ℂ phrasing is what
made the original unelaborable, and nothing in the argument wants it.

Both lift to N: the bound is a computation with no case to decide, and the
representation-count identity is a reindexing. Neither forks because neither has
anywhere to fork.
