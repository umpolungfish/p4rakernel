Add the spectral face: ρ=2 for a pure ring, proved

Isomorphisms.lean carried the algebraic faces; this adds the spectral one
the forge/material reports lean on. Imscribing/SpectralFace.lean proves,
in Mathlib, that a pure head-to-tail ring is 2-regular and its adjacency
matrix carries eigenvalue 2 on the all-ones Perron vector:

  ring_two_regular        every vertex has degree 2
  ring_has_eigenvalue_two ringAdj · 1 = 2 • 1 (the Perron eigenvalue = ρ)
  cyclize_chem_and_math   both faces bound together — the ring closing
                          (chem) IS the all-ones vector at eigenvalue 2 (math)
  branched_row_sum_three  attaching an edge to a genuine non-neighbor lifts
                          a vertex to degree 3 — the ρ>2 signature of branching

Ring adjacency is built as shift + shiftᵀ (a permutation matrix plus its
transpose), so the eigenvalue proof is clean and needs only NeZero n. All
grounded in Mathlib's Fin group + matrix mulVec; no new axioms. Registered
in the lakefile; lake build Imscribing.SpectralFace is green.

Honest scope: the eigenvalue-2 equation on a positive eigenvector is the
Perron value (= ρ); I prove that equation rather than invoking the full
Perron-Frobenius spectral-radius theorem.
