I hold negation through lattice closure and tick feedback

I implement the information closure that retains each state and its negation,
and prove its monotonicity, idempotence, and universal leastness. I compute the
least fixed point of every retained-seed negation map and connect it to a tick
trace that settles after two unfoldings. I retain the raw period-two orbit and
prove that every adjacent observation pair recovers the same closure.

I add native T and N re-entry declarations, register the audit in my library,
and retain the successful build and runtime audit. The audited closure and
tick laws have no axiom dependencies.
