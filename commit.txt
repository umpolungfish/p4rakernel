UNSOLVED, the geometry machinery

IsJordanCurve, IsSquare, IsConvexBody, polar, can_pass_through_L_shape,
kissing_number, IsKakeyaSet, HausdorffDim — none existed. Building them is the
job, so they are built.

A Jordan curve is a continuous periodic map injective on a period. Four points
form a square when the four sides agree, the two diagonals agree, and a diagonal
exceeds a side — the last excluding a degenerate rhombus. A convex body is
convex, compact, with nonempty interior; the polar comes through the inner
product. A Kakeya set carries a unit segment in every direction, and Kakeya is
then full Hausdorff dimension, which Mathlib's dimH gives directly. A kissing
configuration is centres at distance two from the origin, pairwise at distance at
least two — unit balls touching without overlap — and the dimension-five entry is
the bracket that is known, 40 attained and 45 excluded, rather than a disjunction
over five values. The L-corridor and a continuous family of rigid motions give
the moving sofa its own bracket.
