Prove the Liar is the idempotent point: LiarIdempotent.lean

Grounds the sealed-system reading in the tree. On the one Frobenius pair there
are two composites and they do different work. mu after delta is the lossless
round trip, the verification (return_is_lossless). e = delta after mu is the
forgetful idempotent, the Liar point, proved idempotent unconditionally
(e_idempotent) because mu_C output is already special.

The forgetfulness the sealed system needs lives in e, not in the return:
fusion_forgets exhibits two inputs with different parity that fuse to the same
output, so mu_C is non-injective. The return losing nothing and the fusion
forgetting are true at once, of the same pair. fusion_is_time shows every
fusion output is a time fixed point, tying the idempotent point to the time
locus of TimeFixedPoint: the Liar point and the fixed point of time are the
same place. verify_on_image gives co-typing on the image; liar_is_idempotent
bundles it.

Builds green, zero sorries, no native_decide; kernel-checked. Registered in
lakefile.toml.
