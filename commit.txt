The closure theorem, made load-bearing and moved into Imscribing

Two questions, one answer: the root tower is thin AND outside the lakefile for the
same reason, and the reason is that it carries its own Belnap.

There are already two Belnap types in the corpus — Imscribing.Paraconsistent.Belnap
and Paraconsistent.Belnap — and the root files add a third, bare one. That is why
they cannot simply join the lakefile: three Belnaps in one environment is the name
collision that blocks the batch sweep. And the isolation is what let the third copy
DRIFT: its band gives N∧B = N and its bor gives N∨B = B, where the FDE truth-order
meet and join are F and T, which is what the corpus copy has. Two cells wrong in
each table, on the same pair. Everything above it — the classical restriction, the
De Morgan centre — was verified against a non-FDE algebra.

So the closure theorem is rebuilt on the CORPUS Belnap, in
Imscribing/Paraconsistent/IncompletenessClosure.lean, and in the lakefile glob.

Made load-bearing rather than restated. `inc (inc s) = inc s` is true and carries
nothing, since inc is constant and every constant map is idempotent. The claim is
that inc is a CLOSURE OPERATOR on the information order: inflationary, monotone,
idempotent. The first leg is the content and it cannot even be STATED without the
order — it says augmenting a system with its own Gödel sentence moves it UP the
approximation order and never down. Idempotence becomes the leg you get for free,
and inc_image_is_B says so in the file so nobody has to rediscover why.

With the sharpenings the thin version could not reach: inc_fixed_point_iff carries
both directions instead of asserting the easy half; inc_strict_below_B says every
non-B state strictly gains information, which is what fails for an operator fixing
more than one point; inc_closes_in_one_step is strictly stronger than idempotence
and distinguishes this from a closure with a longer ascent; unique_closed_state is
the bridge to the bifurcation law.

Nine declarations, F 0, B 0, T 5, N 4, every one clean — and #print axioms reports
no dependence on any axiom at all, not even the standard three.

The three older root files still carry the divergent algebra and are NOT moved;
they need reproving against the corpus Belnap before they can be, and that is a
separate pass.
