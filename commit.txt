SIC_D2048_Moduli: ramification corrected, vacuous axioms removed, calibration added

The module carried a docstring saying conductor 2 is ramified in the base field
while m2048_val2 in the same file proves padicValNat 2 4190205 = 0, which is
exactly the statement that it is not. 2 is inert: it does not divide the
discriminant, and the single prime above it has residue degree 2. The degree is
unchanged at k=1 because (O/2)^* is cyclic of order 3, odd, so it has no image in
a 2-group quotient. The tower proper begins at k=2. The distinction is load
bearing, since the conductor rule is an exponent on the prime above 2, and a
ramified 2 would give p2^2 = (2) and shift every exponent in it.

tail_stabilization and zauner_cubic_subfield_2048 were axioms of True. True is
provable, so they asserted nothing while counting as axioms and reading as
claims. The first now has content, stated over a wideRayTail function: the tail
is (8,2) for k from 4 to 15. The second is demoted to prose, being a conjecture
with nothing yet to state, and carries the observation that at d=8 the Zauner
eigenspaces have dimensions 3, 2, 3 with the SIC in the two-dimensional one, so a
cubic subfield is not the only shape the eigenspace structure can take.

The larger gap was that k=12 was named the fiducial field by definition, with no
evidence selecting it. Section 8 supplies the calibration: the moduli field
identified outright at d = 4, 8 and 12 by solving the overlap conditions inside a
Zauner eigenspace, refining to four hundred digits, recognising the moduli
algebraically, and reading the conductor off with rnfconductor. Each is the full
ray class field with both infinite places unramified, which is what the moduli
being real numbers forces. calibration_exponent_rule proves the exponent of the
prime above 2 is v_2(d)+1 in all three. d4_d8_share_base_but_differ_in_exponent
proves d = 4 and d = 8 share the base field 5 and are separated by the exponent
alone, which refutes a constant exponent and is what makes the calibration sharp
rather than accommodating. predicted_exponent_2048 gives 12.

The scope note is content, not hedging. Every calibration dimension has class
number one, where the full ray class field and the ray class field modulo the
Hilbert class field coincide and the distinction is empty. At d = 2048 the class
number is 64. Which of the two the moduli field is remains open, and the two
differ by a factor of 64 in the degree. Dimension 16 has class number 2 and would
decide it by the same route.

Builds clean, 8026 jobs, no sorries, and no axiom that asserts nothing.
