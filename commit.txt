Two Erdős files close; one corpus verdict was wrong

SchutteTClose.lean, in the build and sorry-free. Five theorems on the two
classical bound functions: szekeresBound is Θ(n·2ⁿ) with limit 1/2,
probabilisticUpperBound is O(n²·2ⁿ), their ratio is Θ(n) with constants 3 and
12, and R(n)/(6n) → 1. The gap between the classical bounds is exactly linear
with slope 6. None of that touches f, so the statistical branch moves and the
obstructional branch stays B.

The same file refutes the doubling construction that claimed to determine f
exactly. The recurrence f(n+1)=2f(n)+1 from 3 has closed form 2ⁿ⁺²−1, not
(n+2)·2ⁿ⁻¹−1; the two agree only at n=2. doublingCount 2 = 15 against
szekeresBound 3 = 19, so the construction would place property S₃ on 15
vertices and contradict a value this directory already carries. szekeresBound 1
is 2, not f(1)=3. Each decided by the kernel, not argued.

SquarefreePlusPowerOfTwoClose.lean, likewise green. The progression
1764t + 100 contains no squarefree-plus-power-of-two: 1764 = lcm(9, 49, 4), so
2⁰ leaves a multiple of 9, 2¹ leaves a multiple of 49, and for k ≥ 2 both terms
carry a factor of 4. bad 0 = 100 is the smallest counterexample. Infinitely
many, injective in t, positive lower density at c = 1/3528.

ErdosFormalize recorded SquarefreePlusPowerOfTwo as B, "Erdős conjectured it and
it is open." It is neither: Erdős proved the opposite by covering congruences
and the smallest counterexample is 100. Corrected to F with the refutation
named, verdicts list with it.

Both arrived as prose-correct Lean that did not elaborate — Squarefree applied
to the wrong divisibility, IsTheta.of_bound with four arguments, a Mathlib name
that does not exist, comment blocks written in C. The mathematics was sound in
both and none of the Lean was, which is the argument for the kernel being the
step that decides, not the step that confirms.
