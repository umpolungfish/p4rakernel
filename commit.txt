Girth against chromatic number, held as a range

The fifth ob3ect forks this extremal function into an arm where the construction
and the upper bound coincide and an arm where they leave a gap, and its fuse
keeps both — the first contributing a value, the second an interval. So
GirthRange carries two ends and GirthExact is the case where they meet.

Writing it as a single number would assert an exactness the problem does not
have, which is the collapse this kernel exists to refuse. The entry removed from
this file asked for the limit of n/n.
