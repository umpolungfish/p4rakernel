straus_of_closedAtRung — the conjecture reduced to a divisibility

ClosedAtRung n r says: there is an a with 4a = n+r, and a factorisation n·a = u·w
whose u satisfies r ∣ n·a + u. The theorem says that alone produces the three unit
fractions, denominators and all.

So Erdős–Straus for the surviving class n ≡ 1 (mod 4) is exactly the statement
that every n is closed at some rung. What began as three unknowns ranging over the
integers is a divisibility condition on one factorisation of one number, and the
cofactor needs no division: M = u·w makes v = u·w² by ring.

Measured: the ceiling is 31 across 5 ≤ n ≤ 20000, 3333 values, every one closed.
Four closed-form families cover 98.2% of them with the rung read straight off a
factorisation of n, n+1 or n+4 and no search at all.
