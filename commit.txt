Pasch configurations, and the pattern the entry never named

The eighth ob3ect reads this as a forward morphism from a system to the set of
its four-block subsets, forked on whether any matches the pattern. The pattern is
what was missing: four blocks on six points, each point in exactly two of the
four.

IsSteinerTriple, IsPasch and paschCount write it out, and PaschExtremes states
the growth of the extremes with both halves — the bound over every system, and a
system attaining each end. Anti-Pasch systems are the case lo = 0, and their
existence is the part that is not free.

The entry removed here compared c*n with n^2 and named no system.
