Erdos-Straus: k=2 as a residue condition on 2n+1

closedAtRung_of_two_shift. A divisor w = 5 (mod 8) of 2n+1 closes 4/n at the rung
(w+1)/2, and nothing else needs checking: w is odd so coprime to 8, and the
residue is exactly what makes (w+1)/2 = 3 (mod 4).

SurvivesKShift names what a frontier value must satisfy to get past every k up to
K — one multiplicative condition per k, on top of the four already recorded. The
frontier is cut by a growing family of independent conditions, not one
obstruction. n = 193 shows the k=2 condition can hold: 2n+1 = 387 = 9*43, whose
divisors are 1, 3, 9, 43, 129, 387, none of them 5 (mod 8).
