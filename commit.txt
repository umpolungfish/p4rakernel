The bound's route, from the twelfth object

The object designed for the least closing rung reads its reverse morphism as the
product's SIZE forcing the divisor: as M grows its divisors multiply, and a
residue class cannot stay empty once enough of them land. That is the coverage
the instrument has been measuring, and it is the bridge from a measurement to a
bound.

closedAtRung_of_full_coverage proves the bridge: if the divisors of M^2 meet
every class mod r, one of them meets -M and the rung closes.
CoverageReachesFull states what a bound needs on top — that coverage becomes full
by a stated rung — which the measurement supports and does not prove.

All twelve objects have landed. Every one became a definition or a theorem.
