ROTAT: op-opcode proved in the kernel

Imscribing/RotatOpOpcode.lean defines rotat as List.rotate and proves the
automorphism facts: it preserves length, composes additively (ROTAT^m after
ROTAT^n = ROTAT^(n+m)), has order dividing the word length (rotat by |w| = id),
and is a permutation of the word. The permutation fact is the kernel statement
that every multiset and spectral invariant is ROTAT-invariant, the signal that
ROTAT is a symmetry of the ring, not a transformation of its contents.
Registered in the lakefile globs; typechecks clean against the built deps.
