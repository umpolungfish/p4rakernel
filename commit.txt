Two Erdős papers get their proofs; one corpus verdict was stale

SchutteTClose.lean, in the build and sorry-free. Five theorems on the two
classical bound functions: szekeresBound is Θ(n·2ⁿ) with limit 1/2,
probabilisticUpperBound is O(n²·2ⁿ), their ratio is Θ(n) with constants 3 and
12, and R(n)/(6n) → 1. The gap between the classical bounds is exactly linear
with slope 6 against the constant 3, or 2·ln 2 against the sharp constant. None
of it touches f, so the statistical branch moves and the obstructional branch
stays B.

The same file refutes the doubling construction that claimed to determine f
exactly. The recurrence f(n+1)=2f(n)+1 from 3 has closed form 2ⁿ⁺²−1, not
(n+2)·2ⁿ⁻¹−1; the two agree only at n=2. doublingCount 2 = 15 against
szekeresBound 3 = 19, so the construction would place property S₃ on 15
vertices and contradict a value this directory already carries.

SquarefreePlusPowerOfTwo.lean gains its paper-facing statements:
squarefree_plus_pow_two_certificate, hundred_is_the_canonical_obstruction and
counterexample_density_explicit restate the result with NotRepresentable,
SquarefreePlusPowerOfTwo and CounterexampleDensity all unfolded, so the sentence
the kernel checks and the sentence the manuscript asserts are the same sentence
rather than two joined by a definition the reader must trust.

ErdosFormalize recorded SquarefreePlusPowerOfTwo as B, "Erdős conjectured it and
it is open", while the refutation sat in the same repository and the completion
checklist already called it complete. Three documents, two of them right. The
verdict is now F: the universal form fails at N = 100, the least counterexample,
and Erdős proved the opposite by covering congruences.
