The metal reproduces the calibration, and one cost divergence is open

mOMonadOS now carries the lifted words and returns N T B B from its own kernel —
the same check::word_verdict that gates the vita trunk — matching the host. The
calibration verdicts can be booted rather than trusted, which is what the crystal
is for.

The nest does not reproduce there yet, and the reason is worth more than the
result. On the closure law's own proof the host auditor returns T in about eleven
milliseconds; the on-board kernel did not return inside ten minutes. Same
condition, same word, two implementations, agreeing on every small case and
differing by orders of magnitude on a large one. The ancestry pairing is the
suspect. Recorded as an open divergence, not smoothed over.

Also tabulated: verdicts on ten selected theorems. Nine confirmed an expectation.
The tenth did not, and is the useful one — List.reverse_reverse came back N where
T was expected, and inspection of the proof TERM shows it is a rewrite chain
delegating its induction to a separate lemma rather than performing it. The
verdict was right and the expectation was wrong, which is the distinction the
instrument claims to draw.
