The Ramsey number of a pair, from the object that types it

The ob3ect designed for "the least vertex count forcing a monochromatic copy of
one of two given graphs" reads it as a search under a terminal anchor: the
candidates open at FSPLIT, each is tested against every colouring, FFUSE collects
those that force, and IFIX fixes the least. That is the object four removed
statements needed, so it is written now.

MonoCopy says what a monochromatic copy is, ArrowsPair says what forcing means,
RamseyFinite says the search terminates — Ramsey's theorem, cited rather than
proved — and ramseyPair takes the infimum. The three are separate because an
infimum over a possibly empty set is zero rather than "no such order", which is
the junk-value shape this corpus has been bitten by before.

arrowsPair_mono is proved and depends on no axioms: a colouring of the larger
complete graph restricts to the smaller, and a copy found there is a copy here.
