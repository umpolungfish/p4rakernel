The uniformity lift, from the object that forked it on parity

The third ob3ect reads stepping-up as a parity fork with a forward morphism: odd
and even uniformity need different constructions, and what crosses between them
is a blow-up carrying a colouring that avoids a monochromatic set at r to one
that avoids it at r+1.

HyperArrows says what arrowing at uniformity r means, SteppingUpLift says the
lift exists, and HypergraphTowerGrowth says what iterating it gives — a tower
below the Ramsey number. The five entries that stood here asserted that growth
while mentioning no hypergraph; now the growth is stated about the object that
has one.
