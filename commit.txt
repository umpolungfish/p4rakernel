The prover's scratch modules leave the library alone

Imscribing.Scratch.ProverScratch and …B were inside the Imscribing glob while
the kernel-gated prover writes candidate proofs into them, so every prover run
turned `lake build` red until I restored the file by hand — twice today. A red
library that means nothing is worse than no signal.

They have their own lean_lib now, outside the default target. Checked both ways:
with the scratch deliberately broken `lake build` is green at 8591 jobs, and
`lake build Imscribing.Scratch.ProverScratch` still resolves and reports the
failure, which is the output the prover reads.
