Prove stages 5-8 are order-free: GateOrdering.lean section 8

The gate ordering 4<9<12 is forced; the remaining open piece was whether the
four gate-free stages 5 (Putrefaction, F), 6 (Congelation, K), 7 (Cibation,
G) and 8 (Sublimation, Grammar) genuinely float or hide a precedence. They
float, and now it is proved rather than hoped.

Each gate-free stage is a single-axis projection toward the Stone on an axis
no unlock predicate reads. gate_free_commute proves the four pairwise commute
(updates on disjoint axes, all six orders agree). The four *_inert_traced
theorems plus gate_free_inert_frobenius prove each is inert on the gates: it
neither opens nor closes one, so it can be interleaved anywhere. The contrast
is the point: parity_stage_couples shows a gate stage is NOT inert — setting
the parity axis flips unlockTraced from false to true — which is exactly why
the gates are ordered while 5-8 are free. gate_free_order_freedom bundles it.
