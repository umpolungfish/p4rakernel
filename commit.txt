README: record what counts as a clean build, and what stands in the way

No errors is not clean. Clean means no warnings and none silenced: a
set_option disabling a linter does not clear a finding, it hides it.

The note lists the categories standing between the tree and a clean build,
heaviest first, and says which are mechanical and which are not. Line length
and layout are safe to reflow. native_decide is not mechanical, since the
objection is that it trusts the compiler rather than the kernel, so each site
is a decision about what the proof rests on. Unused variables and unused simp
arguments each mark a hypothesis a statement asks for and its proof never
touches, which is worth reading before deleting.

Sorries are named separately because they are not defects to be cleared. Each
is an original claim standing unproven, and the warning reports that
correctly. They close by being proven, never by suppression and never by
weakening the statement until it goes through.
