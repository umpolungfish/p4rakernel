Erdos batch 1: the statements now state their problems

Thirty Props stood in ErdosFormalize.lean. Most did not say what their docstring
said. RamseyOddCycleTriangleLimit was "there is a real strictly between 0 and 1".
ErdosUnbounded was Archimedes. HopfPannwitz was s.card <= s.card. Wagner and
Cumulative compared n, log n and n^2 with a free constant.
RodlShelahChromaticIntersection was proved, as "for every k there is n >= k".

Nine are gone. Five because the faithful statement already lives elsewhere —
Hopf-Pannwitz and Erdos-Fuchs in their proof modules, the odd-cycle Ramsey ratio
proved outright in Erdos/RamseyAsymptotics — and a second weaker copy is drift.
Four because the docstring named no object to restate, or because the subject is
a consistency statement rather than a Prop with a truth value here.

The rest are restated with the object they are about: Ramsey bounds through an
Arrows relation on colourings, the unit-distance and distinct-distance counts as
definitions, upper density and the difference set as definitions, practical
numbers, B3 sets, and Erdos-Hajnal with its conclusion restored.

The verdict table is rebuilt from what the file defines, so it counts what is
here. Library green at 8592 jobs.
