Erdos-Straus: the ladder loses nothing

ladder_complete and ladder_cross. Given any representation 4/n = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z,
put r = 4x - n and M = nx: then u = ry - M and v = rz - M satisfy uv = M^2 and
both congruences, because (M+u)(M+v) = r^2yz = M((M+u)+(M+v)). So the rung and
the divisor are a coordinate system on the representations, not a method for
finding some of them, and a value with no rung has no representation at all.

threeUnit_of_closedAtRungSq is the converse. ClosedAtRungSq carries both
congruences now — the version that took the second as a hypothesis had the
hypothesis doing the work of v_condition_free, which is the defect shape I keep
finding elsewhere.
