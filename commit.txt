E8G2: the duplicate is gone and the workaround with it

E8G2_Vessel_Witnesses declared g2_vessel, e8_aether, e8_graded_via_SO16 and the
two tensor facts in namespace Millennium.E8G2 — the same names, in the same
namespace, that E8G2_Vessel already declares, and it did not import it. The
definitions are identical field for field; only a comment differs. So the two
modules could not be loaded together at all.

That had been met before and papered over: NS_CriticalBound carried a commented
import of the witnesses with the note "(duplicate, content from E8G2_Vessel)". The
collision was known, worked around locally, and left in the tree, so it went on
blocking anything that wanted both.

Witnesses now imports Vessel and drops the five redeclarations, NS_CriticalBound
imports the witnesses again, and the two modules load in one environment. Build
unchanged.

Also repaired eight symlinks under Ob3ects that resolved nowhere. Seven had one
too few levels of `..` — the targets were present all along, one directory further
up than the link said — and are repointed. The eighth, a scaffold generated from a
`__pycache__` directory, has no target and is removed; a generator walked a place
it should not have.
