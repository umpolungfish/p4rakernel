Erdos-Straus: the ceiling is 59 to a million

Extending the read to 10^6, the least closing rung never exceeds 59, first
reached at 118801. The ratio to n^(1/4) falls from 4.6 at 1201 to about 2.0 in
the last block, so the fourth-root law overestimates the growth rather than
fitting it. The docstring now says the measured thing.
