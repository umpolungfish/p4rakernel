Erdos-Straus: the budget gap is steady, not concentrated

The 50000-200000 block shows 21 gaps in 850 rungs walked, spread over many
values, against 19 in 379 below 50000. So it is a persistent mode of a few per
cent, and my reading of it as concentrated on 2521 came from one block. What is
exceptional about 2521 is failing that way at every rung, which is why its
divisor lives in M^2; elsewhere the gap costs one rung and the walk continues.
