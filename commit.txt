Erdos-Turan on additive bases: the counting half proved, the gap named.

This does not settle the conjecture. It proves everything on one side of it and
states what remains as a proposition rather than a gesture, so the boundary is
in the file and not in a paragraph about the file.

bounded_reps_forces_density. If A is an asymptotic basis of order two whose
representation function is bounded by C, then

    N + 1 <= N0 + C * |A cap [0,N]|^2

for every N >= N0. Each integer in the window needs a representation, none has
more than C, and every representation is a pair drawn from the window. The
interval embeds in the image of the sum map over that product, so counting the
pairs both ways gives it. A counterexample must therefore be of maximal counting
order, at least sqrt(N/C).

BoundedRepBasisIsSidonLike. The step that is NOT proved, stated as a definition:
a basis of bounded representation function contains, in each window, a Sidon
sub-structure carrying a constant fraction of it. That is the conjecture's
content and naming it is the honest form of a reduction.

bounded_rep_basis_squeezed. Given that step and the Sidon upper bound, such a
basis is caught between two estimates, dense from below by the counting and thin
from above by the Sidon cap. Both hypotheses are used: an earlier draft carried
them and proved its conclusion without them, which would have dressed the
counting half as a reduction it is not, so the statement was changed to one that
needs them.

The choice function came out along the way. Bounding by the image of the sum map
needs no choice and is shorter than choosing a representation per integer.
