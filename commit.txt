The detector matched the spelling, not the axiom

Swept the corpus and got eight B verdicts, every one of them in the Barriers
module — the one module whose SUBJECT is undischarged claims. That is the shape of
a false positive, and it was one.

isSorry tested whether a declaration's NAME contains the substring "sorry".
`sorryDepth` is a function counting them per problem;
`ym_has_stacked_not_parallel_sorries` is a theorem comparing two of those counts.
Both are fully proved, neither holds an undischarged claim, and both lifted to an
unpaired fork on the strength of their names. The detector was reading the label
on the jar.

It now matches the axiom of unproved assertion and nothing else. Barriers re-lifts
to thirty N and ten T with no B, and the calibration is unchanged: a development
that is one undischarged claim still lifts to a lone fork and still reads B.

Worth stating plainly, since the instrument is the thing being argued for. This
was caught by running it over a corpus large enough to contain its own adversary —
the corpus supplied a module about sorries, and that is what caught a
sorry-detector matching on the word "sorry".
