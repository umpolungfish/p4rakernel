Prove the gate ordering 4 < 9 < 12: Imscribing/GateOrdering.lean

The Alchemical Theory of Mathematics rests on one ordered claim: the twelve
structural invariants must be fixed in a definite order, forced by three
categorical gates at stages 4 (Parity, special Frobenius mu-delta=id), 9
(Criticality, traced monoidal), and 12 (Protection, idempotent terminal). I
had only asserted that order in the manuscript; now it is a theorem.

GateOrdering.lean models the four operad layers as a linearly ordered
inductive (plain < frobenius < traced < terminal) and builds the G2 trace on
the existing polarization Frobenius pair delta_C/mu_C. The hinge is
trace_closes_iff_special: the traced layer is EXACTLY the special class
pol=or and crit=monad, so a closing trace forces the Frobenius value —
traced_needs_frobenius gives 4 < 9 as a theorem, not a definition. The
precedence chain terminal -> traced -> frobenius is proved, and crucially
every inclusion is STRICT: w_frob has the Frobenius value but its trace does
not close, w_traced closes its trace but has no winding to seal the terminal.
The tower does not collapse. g1_is_a_real_barrier re-exports
frobenius_not_synthesizable: or cannot be reached by tensoring lower parities,
so stage 4 is a genuine gate. gate_ordering_theorem bundles the lot.

Builds green against Mathlib v4.28.0, zero sorries, and no native_decide —
every proof is kernel-checked. Registered in lakefile.toml.
