Erdos-Straus: k is a readout, not an axis

Searching over k was the wrong direction. Every cofactor hands back its own
k = (w+1)/r, so reading from the rung side gives the whole k-spectrum of an n
without factoring k*n+1 for any k. Over the 624 frontier values below 200000 the
least k is at most 2 for 314 of them, at most 8 for 565, at most 32 for 613, and
the tail reaches 12813 at n = 66529, rung 135.

With rungs allowed to 200, exactly one value below 200000 has no cofactor of M at
any rung and genuinely needs a divisor of M^2: 2521. 196561 only needed a rung
past 51.
