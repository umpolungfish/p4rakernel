The source states these against an API Mathlib does not have — dominationNumber,
IsHamiltonian, IsPlanar, totalChromaticNumber, unitDistanceGraph and a dozen
more. Naming a constant that does not exist is not a weaker statement, it is no
statement, so the notions are defined here and the conjectures said about them.

HasCliqueMinor is a minor model — disjoint connected branch sets with an edge
between every two — which is what "has K_k as a minor" means and needs no
contraction operation. Hadwiger is then stated as: no k-colouring gives a
K_{k+1} minor. The source asked that the chromatic number be at least the clique
number, which is a theorem, since a clique needs its own vertices coloured apart.

Also: dominating sets and Vizing's product bound, reconstruction above three
vertices, vertex-transitivity and a Hamiltonian cycle for Lovasz,
Erdos-Faber-Lovasz, total colouring over the sum of vertices and edges, and the
unit-distance graph with Hadwiger-Nelson as the interval that remains — not
5-colourable, 7-colourable.
