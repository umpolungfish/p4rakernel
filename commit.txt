The packing density, with its definedness attached

The sixth ob3ect forks on validity — discs pairwise disjoint or some pair
overlapping — and its forward morphism is the passage from a finite window to the
asymptotic proportion by enlarging the window. Its fuse carries the proportion
only when the packing is valid AND the limit exists.

So PackingDensityIs asserts a limit rather than naming a value: a packing whose
density does not converge satisfies it for no d. HexagonalPackingOptimal then has
both halves, the bound and its attainment, because a bound without attainment is
not optimality. The entry that stood here said there is a real strictly between
zero and one, and it is back in the verdict table now that it says something.
