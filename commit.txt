LCMSieve prints its axiom cost

The three results the lcm manuscript rests on now print their dependencies, which
come back as propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound — what the paper claims. A
paper that names its dependencies has to be able to check them.

Sweeping the whole Erdos corpus: 100 axiom lines, 94 on the standard three. The
six that are not are compiled evaluations — the K6 colouring sweep, the SDR
table and its four-window failure, the Schutte lower bound, Landau's table, and
the quintuple search below sixty — and every one of them is declared as compiled
evaluation in its manuscript.
