SerpentRod: three theorems restated to what is true, and the witnesses that show it

All three central claims in SerpentRod.lean were false as written, each carrying
a sorry that could never close because the statement did not hold.

frobenius_serpent_rod asserted mu_reconstruct ∘ delta_forget = transcribeToAA
unrestricted, and its docstring conceded the gap while asserting past it: "using
.U default selects the pyrimidine family, which is consistent with the genetic
code's structure (same AA family, different specific AA)". A different specific
amino acid is exactly what makes the equality fail. delta_forget drops position
3 and mu_reconstruct refills it with U, which selects the pyrimidine member of a
split box, so AUG carries Met on the way in and Ile on the way out.

Restated as the two statements that are true:

- frobenius_serpent_rod_exact: on the exact stratum, where every codon sits in a
  four-fold box, mu ∘ delta IS the identity on the amino acid sequence. Proved by
  induction, discharged at each codon by frobenius_at_codon_level, which was
  already there.
- frobenius_serpent_rod_fails_split: AUG is a counterexample to the unrestricted
  form, by decide.

split_collapse_pairs names the collapse: each split box whose halves carry
different amino acids sends its purine member to its pyrimidine member.
collapsing_pairs_are_promoted records that both members of every such pair are
promoted, so mu ∘ delta maps twelve primitive-bearing amino acids onto a strictly
smaller set. The Frobenius holds on the ground layer and fails exactly where the
Grammar's twelve primitives live.

serpent_winding_bounds_contacts and direct_mapping_closure asserted inequalities
between winding number and predicted contacts in opposite directions. Both are
false, and one witness refutes both: three Ser codons, UCUUCUUCU. Ser is ground
layer, so it activates no primitive and the fold has no contacts, while the
nucleotide path still winds three times. Winding is read off the raw path,
contacts are counted between promoted residues at distance four or more, and the
two are independent rather than ordered. Replaced by
winding_does_not_bound_contacts and contacts_do_not_bound_winding, each proved
from the witness, plus no_promoted_no_contacts for the part that does hold.

transcribeToAA, delta_forget and mu_reconstruct kept their behaviour; their inner
let rec bodies were lifted to top-level transcribeGo, deltaGo and muToAA so the
induction could name them.

SerpentRod.lean now has no sorries.
