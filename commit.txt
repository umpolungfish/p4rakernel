Erdos-Straus: the rung reads off k*n+1

Every cofactor is w = k*r - 1, so the rung is (w+1)/k and the condition w | M
becomes a condition on n alone: 4k*a = k*n + w + 1, so w | k*n+1 with w coprime
to 4k gives w | a. That is closedAtRung_of_kshift.

This removes the circularity. The divisor search wanted a factor of M = n*a while
a depended on the rung being tested; now each k needs only the factorisation of
k*n+1, and k=1 is the divisor family already there. Below 20000 the frontier's 83
values fall to k <= 8 in 64 cases and to k <= 40 in 74. Only even k occur, since
w must be odd where M is.
