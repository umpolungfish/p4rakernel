q817: the base-3 construction, machine-checked

The sumset-avoiding-k-APs answer was prose in a docstring. It rejects
g_3(n) >> 3^n by the construction A = {3^0,…,3^(n-1)}, whose subset sums are
exactly the numbers with base-3 digits in {0,1}, none three of which form an
arithmetic progression. Elementary, and there was no reason for it to stay
unchecked while the file around it carried 0 sorries.

no_three_ap now proves it: for digits in {0,1}, x + z = 2y forces x = y = z, at
every length n. no_nondegenerate_three_ap is the corollary that distinctness is
outright impossible. Both on propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound only.

The mechanism is no carries, and it is what makes the induction go. Digits in
{0,1} give x%3 + z%3 <= 2 and 2*(y%3) <= 2, so reading x + z = 2y modulo 3 is an
EQUALITY and not merely a congruence: nothing carries into the next place, the
AP condition descends one digit at a time, and at each place it forces
x_i = y_i = z_i.
