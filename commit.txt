Two vacuous axioms constructed away, and one kept for a reason

paralogical_copy and paralogical_reflect were not false, they were empty. Both
types are inhabited by construction and both hypotheses go unused: `arrow`
builds a protocol between any two imscriptions, so a labelled self-arrow gives
depth 1 and a prod of two gives max 1 1 = 1. Neither `dim = if'` nor
`tier = O_inf` is what makes the witness exist. They are defs now, witness
constructed, signatures unchanged so the 42 ob3ect scaffolds still compile.

The finding is what the demotion exposes rather than what it removes. If the
intended claim is that delta is a genuine Frobenius comultiplication rather than
any depth-1 protocol, that is a stronger statement and it is not written
anywhere. Same for imscriptive self-reference against an arbitrary self-arrow:
"depth >= 1" does not tell them apart. An axiom that asserts nothing is worse
than a missing one, because it looks like the claim has been made.

paralogical_dagger stays an axiom and I nearly demoted it wrongly. A trivial
witness inhabits the reversal type, so it reads vacuous like the other two — but
paralogical_dagger_depth demands the reversal PRESERVE depth, and there is no
structural construction: prod f g : IGProtocol a (b ⊗ c) reverses to
IGProtocol (b ⊗ c) a, and no constructor assembles that from the two reversed
branches. Real content. The reason is now in the source so nobody removes it on
the strength of the trivial witness.

Eight axioms, three defects. A free variable in the consequent (A, B, C, D):
asserts the conclusion of every value. A vacuously true hypothesis (bowtie,
box): holds for every n. An inhabited type with unused hypotheses (copy,
reflect): asserts nothing at all. The first two prove False. The third is
quieter and, on this evidence, more common.
