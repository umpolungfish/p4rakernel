The banking rule is formalized, and the two facts are proved independent.

The rule kept being restated as one thing and it is two, which is why every
version had a counterexample.

WHETHER ANYTHING IS BANKED depends only on whether a frame is open when the
clear fires. banking_dichotomy: after a clear, what a fuse returns is exactly
the head of the frame stack, or zero when the stack is empty. Nothing about the
deposits, their count, or the placement of earlier splits enters it. This is
governed by how many fuses have already run, not by where the splits sit.

WHETHER A SURPLUS IS KEPT depends on where the splits fall between deposits of
the same value. same_region_restores_two against sibling_regions_restore_one:
the same two deposits, the same one fuse before the clear, restoring two or one
according only to whether a split separates them. Deposits inside a region
accumulate; the fold between regions takes a maximum.

independence exhibits both failures at once: a word that banks correctly and
still discards a surplus, and a word whose deposits sit in one region and are
lost entirely because its fuse ran before the clear.

Also proved: touch_writes_both, that a deposit lands in the register and the
innermost frame in one action, so the second copy exists before any fuse runs
and the nesting decides where it sits rather than whether it exists; and
register_keeps_both, that the register keeps every deposit however arranged,
since the flattening happens in the frames and never in the open.

Elaborates clean, zero sorries. The set-valued machine is the case where every
weight is 0 or 1, where max is union and all of this degenerates to membership,
which is why the base machine cannot see any of it.
