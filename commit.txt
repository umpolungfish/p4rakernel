The list-chromatic threshold, with the discarded arm restored

The tenth ob3ect forks the graph class on the property and reports B, and its
reason is worth keeping: the fuse stays open because the arm of graphs that do
NOT exceed the bound is dropped when the least order is named. A minimum over one
arm is not a fusion of the split — what went into the fork does not come back out
of it — so the object records the defect instead of hiding it behind a value.

LeastOrderListChromatic therefore carries both halves: a bipartite witness at the
order, and the absence of one below it. The second conjunct is the arm the fuse
dropped, put back. ListColourable and ListChromaticExceeds write out the notion
the removed entry never mentioned, its conclusion having been that some N is
positive.
