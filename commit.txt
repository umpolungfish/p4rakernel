straus_master — the surviving class from one divisor

The pieces compose. Fix n ≡ 1 (mod 4) and a rung r ≡ 3 (mod 4); put a = (n+r)/4
and M = n·a. Then ONE divisor u of M² with r ∣ M+u gives

    4/n = 1/a + 1/(M+u)/r + 1/(M+v)/r,     v = M²/u

The rung supplies the first term, the divisor supplies the other two, and the
second congruence the raw criterion asks for is free (v_condition_free).

That is the surviving class of Erdős–Straus reduced to a divisor search on a
single number per rung: a finite decidable question for each n, where the
original statement has three unknowns ranging over the integers.
