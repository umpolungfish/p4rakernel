Erdos-Straus: the shift family, and the frontier is now essentially primes

Any divisor d of n divides M = n*a, so a rung r = 3 (mod 4) dividing d+1 closes
by the cofactor form. The rung is read off the factorisation of n alone, a is
never consulted, and it is not of monomial type — the exhaustion theorem does not
reach it. That is closedAtRung_of_shift, and ShiftCovered now sits in the
frontier definition beside PriceZeroCovered.

It halves the frontier: 1271 values below 200000 become 638, and multiplicative
descent takes it to 624, of which 622 are prime. The composites survive only when
every factor does, which is what descent means.

202 at 7, 55 at 11, down to the same two values needing a divisor of M^2.
