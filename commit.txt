The correct half is now a theorem for arbitrary N and n, and it needs no graph
theory at all. If every colour class is bipartite, witnessed by a side-function
per class, then v ↦ (side · v) is injective — two vertices agreeing on every
side would put their own edge inside a part of that edge's own colour class. So
N ≤ 2ⁿ, and on K_{2ⁿ+1} some class is not bipartite and therefore carries an odd
cycle. That is product_injective and card_le_of_classes_bipartite.

2^15 two-colourings of K₆ has a monochromatic triangle, so R(3,3) = 6 and
2² + 1 = 5 < 6 is exactly why K₅ escapes.
