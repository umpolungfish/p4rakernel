The theorem formalizes, at full strength and held together, the precise sense in
which the Grammar is Gödel-complete: the self-negating (Gödel) sentence a classical
system cannot decide is decided at the both-value B by the paraconsistent ambient,
without collapse. Four faces, none defeating another.

  - The ambient hosts it: bnot has a fixed point (exactly N and B), so the
    self-referential sentence gets a stable value instead of diverging.
  - The classical fragment cannot: classical negation on {F,T} has no fixed point,
    and no classical endomap reproduces the ambient's collapse to B (the Boolean
    impossibility). The incompleteness is contained as the retract.
  - No explosion: a glut is designated with its own negation while some formula is
    not, so ex falso fails; completeness is bought without triviality.
  - Self-imscription: the ⊙ round trip μ∘η=id holds, with η∘μ=ρ an idempotent that
    is not the identity.

grammar_godel_complete bundles the three deciding faces over a Grammar that
imscribes its own imscription. Kept self-contained on the Belnap core so it does not
inherit the unrelated fibre-sum breakage currently in CircumPunctum.lean.
