Prove the general fibre identity in CircumPunctum

The fibre identity ∑ₖ C(m,k) F(k) = 2^(2^m) now holds for every m, not just the
concrete levels. F_fibre is the alternating binomial transform of 2^(2^i), and the
identity is binomial inversion.

Proof structure, all in the kernel:
  - alt_choose_column: ∑ₖ (-1)^(k-i) C(m,k) C(k,i) = [i = m], from the trinomial
    revision C(m,k)C(k,i) = C(m,i)C(m-i,k-i) (Nat.choose_mul) and the vanishing
    alternating row sum ∑ⱼ (-1)^j C(m-i,j) (Int.alternating_sum_range_choose).
  - binomial_inversion: push C(m,k) inside, extend the inner range (C(k,i)=0 above
    the diagonal), swap the double sum, apply the column collapse, and read off the
    diagonal term a m.
  - fibre_identity: instantiate the inversion at a i = 2^(2^i).

CircumPunctum builds green, sans sorry; fibre_identity rests on the standard Mathlib
base (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound).
