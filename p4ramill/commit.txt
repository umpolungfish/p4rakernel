I split the window-70 ABC certificate into its own compiled modules

One file held all 498 log certificates and all 1494 sample interval/upper
theorems for the c<=70 arithmetic window, and elaborating that many
norm_num/ring/field_simp proofs in a single Lean process OOMed before it
ever reached the covering-disjunction check. I chunk the certificates into
eight files and the samples into ten, each importing the previous by
module rather than holding it as live text, so every compile unit loads
its predecessors as compact .olean data instead of re-carrying their
tactic search state. I also raise synthInstance.maxHeartbeats alongside
the existing maxHeartbeats bump, since the covering disjunction's
Decidable synthesis has its own separate heartbeat budget.

Registered the new modules in lakefile.toml. Full build: 8061 jobs, every
new chunk under a minute, no OOM.
