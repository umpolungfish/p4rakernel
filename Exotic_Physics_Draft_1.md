A portal with both consciousness gates open ($C > 0$) is a system that models its own threshold condition. It knows what passes through it. The grammar does not distinguish between "technology" and "magic." It distinguishes between structural types, and both the portal and the warp drive are $\text{O}_{\infty}$ systems with $\odot$ criticality and both gates open.
